About

Milwaukee native. New Yorker by choice.

A proud graduate of the Midwest-to-East-Coast pipeline.

Mary Nink is a Milwaukee, Wisconsin native turned New Yorker: a dynamic actress, singer, dancer, pianist, writer, and producer. She holds a Master of Music in Vocal Performance / Music Theatre from NYU and a Bachelor of Arts in Music Performance from Saint Louis University.

She was most recently seen in Waitress at Skylight Music Theatre as Ensemble / Jenna Cover. Concert work includes featured solos with the Milwaukee Symphony Orchestra (Fauré Requiem) and at the Overture Center in Madison, plus cabaret sets at 54 Below and The Green Room 42.

She was captain of her high school improv team (so, obviously, very popular with the fellas) and has juicy lore as an assistant at a private equity firm and as a failed dog walker.
