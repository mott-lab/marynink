No two voices are alike.

I am a voice and acting coach for students of all ages. Whether you are a high school student preparing for a college audition, an adult rediscovering singing, or anywhere in between, the work starts with your voice and what you want it to do.

With a Master of Music in Vocal Performance from NYU, a Bachelor of Arts from Saint Louis University, and more than ten years of performing, I teach voice, acting, and movement wherever you are. No experience is necessary.

Who it is for

Students

College and conservatory audition prep: repertoire, prescreens, monologues, and the nerves that come with them.

Working singers & actors

Technique maintenance, audition book building, and preparing the voice for a major tour or contract.

Adults & professionals

Rediscover singing, or protect and strengthen a speaking voice: teachers, speakers, fitness instructors.

Offerings

Individual and personalized. A sample, not a limit.

  • Voice training for professional singers and actors
  • Preparing the voice for a major tour
  • Voice and acting coaching for conservatory or college auditions
  • Musical theatre workshops and masterclasses for schools and companies
  • Voice training for adults, speakers, teachers, and fitness instructors
  • Monologue coaching
