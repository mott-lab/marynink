Welcome!

I am a passionate voice and acting coach for students of all ages. Whether you’re a high school student preparing for a college audition, an adult looking to rediscover singing, or any shade in between, I am here for YOU! No two voices are alike, and I look forward to exploring what tools and techniques will help you succeed.

With a Master of Music in Vocal Performance from NYU and a Bachelor of Arts from Saint Louis University, I have extensive training on vocal technique. I leverage my 10+ years of performing experience to provide acting, movement, and voice instruction wherever you are. There is NO experience necessary to work with me. I am excited to bring my knowledge and background to our journey together!

Who it is for

Students

College and conservatory audition prep: repertoire, prescreens, monologues, and the nerves that come with them.

Working singers & actors

Technique maintenance, audition book building, and preparing the voice for a major tour or contract.

Adults & professionals

Rediscover singing, or protect and strengthen a speaking voice: teachers, speakers, fitness instructors.

  • Musical theatre workshops and masterclasses for schools and companies
  • Voice training for adults, speakers, teachers, and fitness instructors
  • Monologue coaching
  • Piano lessons for beginners through intermediate players, all ages
